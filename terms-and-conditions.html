2. Bespoke Commissioning Protocol and Deposit Obligations

Because every Caliber IX split-seconds chronograph requires the dedicated procurement of noble metals like 950 platinum and hundreds of hours of master hand anglage finishing, all bespoke commissions require an advance non-refundable deposit of fifty percent prior to ebauche cutting. The remaining balance, including insured specialized courier fees and applicable state taxes, becomes fully due upon photographic presentation and acoustic timing certification of the completed timepiece prior to final dispatch from our Mercer Street facility.

3. Natural Material Variations and Artisanal Authenticity

Our caliber bridges are crafted from untreated German silver (maillechort) that naturally develops a warm honey patina over consecutive decades of collector ownership. Patrons acknowledge and appreciate that authentic hand-anglage chamfers, flame-blued screw shades, and hand-turned guilloché patterns exhibit subtle microscopic variations that confirm genuine artisan execution. These natural artistic characteristics do not constitute defects but represent the cherished hallmarks of genuine master handcraft horology.

4. Production Timelines and Handcraft Scheduling

Given the exacting nature of free-sprung balance poising, two-needle alligator strap stitching, and fifteen-day chronometer testing across five spatial positions, production intervals range from sixteen to twenty-four weeks depending on atelier commission volume. While our manufacture exerts every reasonable effort to satisfy estimated delivery benchmarks, timeline projections remain subject to the natural assembly phases of high complications, and adjustments shall not constitute a breach of this commission contract.
