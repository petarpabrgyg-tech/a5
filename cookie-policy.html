1. Architectural Foundation and Purpose of Cookies

Rattrapante IX Manufacture Horlogère, based at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, maintains this Cookie Architecture Charter to transparently explain how micro-data files, session tokens, and telemetry scripts function across rattrapanteix.com. Cookies are compact cryptographic text files placed on your computing device when visiting our website, enabling our servers to recognize your browsing preferences, maintain active appointment reservations, and deliver a smooth luxury experience.

2. Categorization of Operational and Technical Cookies

Our digital infrastructure deploys three precise categories of cookie mechanisms: Strictly Necessary Cookies, which sustain secure navigation, shopping bag caching, and CSRF security tokens; Analytical Telemetry Cookies, which measure page load performance, layout responsiveness, and regional visitation trends; and Preference Cookies, which preserve currency selections and language choices across consecutive atelier visits without capturing personal biometric data.

3. Google Analytics Integration and Privacy Telemetry

To evaluate digital portal traffic and optimize navigation across our bespoke portfolio, we deploy Google Analytics via the global gtag.js script (Measurement ID: G-0LY0HY7L01). This diagnostic integration gathers anonymized telemetry including approximate geographic region, browser version, and page dwell duration, while IP anonymization masks your specific physical network address. This statistical data is utilized purely to enhance website loading velocity and mobile responsiveness across devices.

4. Absolute Prohibition of Third-Party Behavioral Tracking

Unlike commercial fast-fashion platforms, Rattrapante IX rejects invasive behavioral advertising networks, retargeting pixels, and cross-site data harvesting brokers. We do not permit external advertising syndicates to embed marketing trackers or surveillance cookies upon our digital properties. Any analytical telemetry collected through our portal remains strictly compartmentalized and is never shared, auctioned, or cross-referenced for aggressive behavioral profiling.

5. Patron Autonomy and Browser Management Controls

Every patron maintains complete sovereign control over cookie deployment upon their personal computing hardware. You may configure your preferred web browser—whether Safari, Chrome, Firefox, or Edge—to block all third-party cookies, alert you before new session tokens are stored, or purge previously cached data upon closing your session window. Modifying these settings will not impair your fundamental ability to explore our bespoke portfolio or schedule salon appointments.

6. Policy Governance, Updates, and Contact Information

We maintain the institutional prerogative to revise this Cookie Architecture Charter as international data privacy directives develop or new security protocols emerge. All modifications become effective immediately upon posting to this portal, accompanied by an updated effective date. If you have questions regarding our telemetry practices or wish to submit cookie preferences, contact our Technical Governance Team at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or phone +1-888-777-5845.
